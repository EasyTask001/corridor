# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

- **Be respectful** — Value different perspectives and experiences
- **Be inclusive** — Welcome newcomers, help them learn
- **Be constructive** — Give actionable feedback; focus on code, not people
- **Be collaborative** — Share knowledge, credit others' work
- **Be responsible** — Own mistakes, learn from them

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Trolling, flame wars, or deliberate disruption
- Publishing private information without consent
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Threats of violence or intimidation
- Spam, self-promotion, or off-topic noise

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate corrective action in response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (GitHub, Discord, email, etc.) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement Guidelines

### 1. Correction

**Impact**: Minor, first offense
**Response**: Private written warning, explanation of violation

### 2. Warning

**Impact**: Repeated or moderate
**Response**: Temporary restriction (e.g., comment moderation, PR review delay)

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Impact**: Serious or persistent
**Response**: Temporary ban from all project spaces (duration communicated)

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Impact**: Severe, harmful, or repeated after temporary ban
**Response**: Permanent removal from all project spaces
