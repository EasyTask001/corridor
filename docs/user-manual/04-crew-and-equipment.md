# Crew and equipment

## Drivers

Licence, medical certificate, citizenship and the documents customs asks for (FAST, passport, visa) each carry an expiry. Alerts warn 60 days ahead and flag anything expired. Drivers can opt in to SMS for ACE and ACI entry notifications and to receiving their driver sheet by e-mail.

## Trucks and trailers

Unit number, VIN, plate and jurisdiction, and the annual inspection, registration and insurance dates. Extra plates for other jurisdictions go in the plate list. Trailers carry the CBP equipment type (dry van, reefer, flatbed, tanker, chassis, step deck) that the manifest transmits.

## Exports

Every registry has **Export CSV** and **Export PDF** in its toolbar; the file is kept under the organization's documents and the link opens it.

## History

**History** on any row lists who changed what and when.
