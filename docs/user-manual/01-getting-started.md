# Getting started

Corridor files ACE (US-bound) and ACI (Canada-bound) truck manifests, tracks what customs answers, and keeps the crew, equipment and partner registries that every filing draws on.

## Signing in

Use the work e-mail your administrator invited. Leave **Stay signed in** checked on your own computer to keep the session across browser restarts; uncheck it on a shared terminal. **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page e-mails a reset link.

## The main menu

- **Dashboard**: alerts, manifests this month, the trailing-year chart and the last shipments touched.
- **Movements**: one manifest per truck crossing, built step by step.
- **Shipments**: every customs filing on its own, whether or not it is on a truck yet.
- **In-bond**, **PARS RNS**: bonded moves and CBSA release notifications.
- **Drivers, Trucks, Trailers, Partners**: the registries.
- **Reports**: the crossing log and free-form questions, both exportable.
- **Resources** and **Help**: outside references and this manual.

## Roles

What you can see and change is set by your role under Settings → Roles. A read-only user sees every list but no Save buttons.
